An AI-powered study planning application designed to help students organize their learning, create personalized study schedules, set academic goals, and manage their study time effectively.

## 📌 About the Project

Students often struggle with managing multiple subjects, assignments, exams, and limited study time.

The *Smart Study Planner with AI* aims to solve this problem by providing a simple and intelligent platform where students can organize their academic activities and generate personalized study plans based on their goals and available time.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 Add and manage subjects
- 🗓️ Create personalized study schedules
- 🤖 AI-assisted study planning
- 🎯 Set academic goals
- ⏰ Manage available study time
- 📊 Track study progress
- 📝 Manage tasks and assignments
- 📱 Responsive and user-friendly interface

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Enter your subjects and academic goals.

4. The application creates a structured study plan.
5. Follow the schedule and track your progress.
